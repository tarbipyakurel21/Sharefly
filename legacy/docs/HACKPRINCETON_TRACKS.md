# HackPrinceton tracks and rules

## Verified event rules

The Devpost rules page says teams may have up to four members, all members must be on the submission, only one of the six main tracks may be selected, sponsor tracks may be added, and each hacker may contribute to only one project. The project must be built during Nov 13, 8:00 PM ET through Nov 15, 8:00 AM ET, use a public GitHub repository created/linked before the deadline, and be presented in person for no more than two minutes during judging.

The six main tracks named in the brief are Healthcare, Sustainability, Business and Enterprise, Entertainment and Media, Education, and Science and Discovery. The public pages did not expose individual descriptions or awards for these tracks; organizer confirmation is pending.

Verified judging dimensions: Creativity, Utility, Charity, and Avidity. Attendance is in person; the website says applicants must be matriculated undergraduate or graduate students.

## Source conflict

The rules body states **Sunday, Nov 15, 2026 at 8:00 AM** is a hard stop. Devpost page metadata displays 8:30 AM, while the supplied brief reports another schedule at 8:45 AM. Use 8:00 AM as the safe internal cutoff and ask organizers to confirm the discrepancy.
